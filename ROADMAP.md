# SignLink AI Roadmap

This document outlines the planned features and improvements for SignLink AI.

## Vision

To become the world's leading AI-powered sign language communication platform, breaking down communication barriers and empowering the deaf and hard-of-hearing community globally.

## Current Status (v1.0.0) ✅

- ✅ Real-time sign language recognition (ASL, ISL, BSL)
- ✅ Web and mobile applications
- ✅ 3D avatar animation for text-to-sign
- ✅ Production-ready infrastructure
- ✅ Multi-tenancy and enterprise features
- ✅ Comprehensive documentation

## 2026 and Beyond

### Long-term Goals 🎯

1. **Universal Sign Language Translation**
   - Support for 50+ sign languages
   - Real-time translation between different sign languages
   - Dialect and regional variation support

2. **Advanced AI Capabilities**
   - Emotion and sentiment analysis
   - Contextual understanding and disambiguation
   - Personalized models that adapt to individual signing styles
   - Real-time sign language generation from audio

3. **Accessibility Leadership**
   - Partnership with accessibility organizations
   - Integration into major platforms (iOS, Android, Windows)
   - Government and institutional adoption
   - Free tier for individuals and non-profits

4. **Research & Innovation**
   - State-of-the-art model architectures
   - Multimodal learning (sign + speech + text)
   - Cross-lingual sign language understanding
   - Contribution to sign language linguistics

5. **Global Impact**
   - 10M+ active users worldwide
   - 100+ supported sign languages
   - Partnerships with deaf communities globally
   - Educational programs in 50+ countries
